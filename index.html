Correct Choice Home Care Affiliation

View the job description for Caregiver / Care Worker

Job summary: The Caregiver provides non-medical home care and companionship to clients in their own homes, supporting older adults and individuals with disabilities to live safely and comfortably while maintaining their independence and dignity.

Key responsibilities:

  • Companionship, light housekeeping, laundry, and meal preparation
  • Personal care assistance (bathing, dressing, grooming) as assigned
  • Medication reminders only — caregivers do not administer or handle medication
  • Errands, transportation coordination, and accompaniment to appointments
  • Activities, games, and crafts; support for clients with dementia or Alzheimer's
  • Reporting changes in a client's condition to the Agency

Caregiver policies: no shaving/razors on clients, no trimming client fingernails/toenails, medication reminders only (never administered), and no travel in the caregiver's or client's own personal vehicle.

Qualifications: GED or High School Diploma minimum, at least 18 years of age, qualified as Bonded and Insurable, and successful completion of a criminal background check.

Schedule: Hours vary by client assignment; the Agency cannot guarantee a set schedule or minimum number of work hours.
